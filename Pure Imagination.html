Project Information

  • Project: Count to Three
  • Category: Fine Arts
  • Medium: Mixed Media
  • Materials: Acrylic Paint & Digital Illustration
  • Digital Process: Procreate & Lightroom
  • Concept: Imagination, Play & Movement
  • Artist: Grace Mena
  • Year: 2026

Project Overview

Count to Three emerged after listening to “Pure Imagination,” performed by Gene Wilder as Willy Wonka in the 1971 film Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory. The song presents imagination as a place of freedom and possibility, and its words immediately made me think about my relationship with imagination as a child.

I remembered how we used to create imaginary worlds together. Each of us would become a character, and within that shared world, anything was possible. It made me think of imagination not only as something individual, but as a space we can create and inhabit with others. I decided to draw while listening to the song, allowing its lyrics and the thoughts they triggered to guide me. Rather than illustrating the song literally, I wanted to see what its words made me imagine and how those ideas would translate visually.
