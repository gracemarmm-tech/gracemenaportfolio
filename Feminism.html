Project Overview

Agentes de Cambio is a year-long political education program by Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung (FES) that seeks to provide young people with the knowledge and tools to become more informed, conscious, and engaged citizens.

Throughout the program, I am responsible for documenting its different modules through photography and videography, capturing both the learning process and the experiences of its participants. I also create and edit audiovisual content to communicate the program’s activities and extend its impact beyond the classroom.

Module II: Feminisms focused on conversations and learning around feminism, becoming part of the ongoing audiovisual record of the program.
