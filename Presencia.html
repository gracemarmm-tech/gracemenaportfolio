Project Information

  • Project: Presence
  • Category: Animation Short Film
  • Medium: Photography & Animation
  • Concept: Presence, Human Identity & Self-Perception
  • Artist: Grace Mena
  • Year: 2024

Project Overview

Presence was inspired by a reflection that emerged after reading Sapiens. After seeing the image of a handprint on a cave wall, I began thinking about how that human and I share the same desire to say: I was here.

This made me think about our need to perceive ourselves and our humanity through what we create. From this idea, I began photographing my hands and used them as the basis for the animation.ally felt like an act of impurity, even when there was nothing inherently impure about what I was doing.

The piece also reflects on my difficulty accepting that growing meant my body would change and that with those changes, people might begin to make different assumptions about me. It questions why growing into one’s body can alter the way purity is perceived and asks: Am I impure simply for growing?

Creative Process

Written Reflection

A human from 300,000 years ago and I are basically the same. We are not that different.

We have shared the same impulse to see, say, and feel: “I am here.”

When I look at cave paintings, they make a lot of sense to me, especially the handprints. I sometimes think that this may have been their way of perceiving themselves, just as I look at myself in a mirror or record myself dancing.

I think humans have a deep fascination with perceiving ourselves and with perceiving others. That is part of why social media, cinema, and theater exist.

Even the things we choose to identify with become ways of seeing ourselves. Having a favorite food, saying that baleadas or pollo chuco are your favorite foods, means choosing something that you want to become part of your representation. Through that representation, you are able to perceive yourself.

The music you listen to, and even the person you are attracted to, can also become ways of perceiving yourself.

In the end, everything around you that you choose to represent you becomes another way of seeing yourself without having to look at yourself directly through someone else’s eyes.
