Project Overview

Solo Aroma y Alma (Only Scent and Soul) was a three-month campaign created for Aroma Candle, a brand centered around authenticity, well-being, and intentional craftsmanship. The goal was to communicate the calm and honesty behind the brand while embracing the raw, imperfect nature of its process.

We began by developing a content strategy and a three-month content calendar aligned with the brand's message and identity. From there, we planned the campaign and produced the audiovisual content needed to bring it to life, including photography and video. Rather than creating an overly polished image, we chose to show the process as it really is: organic, imperfect, and genuine.

Solo Aroma y Alma was created around the idea that the essence of the brand does not come from perfection, but from the care, intention, and soul behind each product.
