  • Performer: Grace Mena

Project Overview

Who Copies Who? is a dance improvisation exploring how consuming art changes the way I exist and interact with the world. Music changes the way I move and carry my body. Films influence the way I dress, behave, and imagine my life. The art I consume becomes part of me, shaping the way I experience and express myself.

We often say that art imitates life, but while creating this piece, I began questioning that relationship. If art is shaped by the way we live, what happens when we begin shaping our lives through art? If art imitates life, why do I imitate art to live mine?
